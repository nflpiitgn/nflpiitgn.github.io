What the program sets out to do

Program objectives

Five aims, from understanding oneself to changing something real in one’s own conduct and in one’s institution.

Help participants examine their values, leadership styles, emotional responses, and use of authority in academic settings.

Develop skills in active listening, supportive conversations, constructive feedback, teamwork, and conflict resolution.

Strengthen judgment when decisions involve uncertainty, power differences, bias, academic expectations, or competing claims of fairness.

Build the capacity to recognize student wellbeing concerns and respond within the faculty role, including appropriate referral.

Enable each participant to identify a feasible change in personal conduct and an institutional practice they can influence.

From aims to practice

Each objective is practised through conversation, art, sport, theatre and reflection across the five residential days.
