Four things worth knowing
No. 01

A riverside campus, built for walking

The 400-acre campus runs along the Sabarmati at Palaj. Its plan encourages walking and cycling, and its landmark is the Lal Minar, the red tower that rises above the trees.

No. 02

A living laboratory for sustainability

IITGN was the first campus in India to receive the 5-star GRIHA LD rating. Buildings use cavity walls, night purge systems and passive shading; solar panels cover rooftops, a carport and a walkway; and the plan aimed for a zero-discharge, zero-waste campus.

No. 03

Students First

The institute puts students first through initiatives such as the Foundation Programme, Invent@IITGN and the Explorers Fellowship. The five-week Foundation Programme for new undergraduates, running since 2011, received the World Education Award in 2013.

No. 04

Practices adopted across the IITs

The IIT Council recommended that IITGN’s exemplary practices in labour welfare and community outreach be introduced in all IITs.
