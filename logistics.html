Structure and participation

Who joins, and how it is funded

The program will run for four cohorts. The details below apply to each cohort.

Cohorts and batch size
Four cohorts, maximum 30 participants each
Cohort dates
December 16–20, 2026 COHORT 1
January 18–22, 2027 COHORT 2
February 15–19, 2027 COHORT 3
March 22–26, 2027 COHORT 4
Duration and mode
Five days (30 hours), residential
Venue
IIT Gandhinagar
Who may be nominated
Deans and Senior faculty of Centrally Funded Institutions (CFIs) who are entrusted or likely to be entrusted with student-facing responsibilities
Nominations per institution
One
Nominating authority
The Head of the Institution (Director/Vice-Chancellor), who nominates the DSW
Funding
The Ministry of Education will fund the program, including boarding and lodging. Travel to and from the host institute will be covered by the participants’ institutions.
The host

A residential campus on the Sabarmati
